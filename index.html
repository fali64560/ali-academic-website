INTRODUCTION

About Me

I am a Civil Engineering master's student interested in computational mechanics, numerical modeling, and multiphysics problems in porous materials. My research interests include coupled thermo-hydro-mechanical processes, fractured porous media, and numerical methods for modeling discontinuities and interfacial phenomena.
